Your private properties

Create and complete your property details privately. Only you can view them. A complete property is still private; publishing is not available yet.

    Edit property details

    The location above is fixed to this property. Save partial details as a draft, or mark the property complete when all required details are ready. Saving as a draft returns a complete property to draft status.

    Up to 2,000 characters. At least 20 are required to mark complete.

    Use 0 bedrooms for a studio. Homes and apartments need at least 1 bathroom to be complete. Bathrooms are optional for commercial properties; rooms do not apply to land.

    Enter prices in Ethiopian birr and areas in square metres, with up to two decimal places. A positive price and area are required to mark complete.

    Property photos

    Photos remain private. Add up to 10 JPEG, PNG, or WebP photos, each up to 8 MiB. Images are resized and converted to JPEG; embedded metadata is removed. The first photo is the cover.

      Photo uploads, ordering, and removal save immediately. They do not save or discard changes in your property details above. Removing a photo permanently removes it from this property.

      English and Amharic listing assistant

      With your permission, AkGebeya sends this property's saved title, description, property type, rent or sale choice, price, area, room counts, and subcity to Google Gemini to generate English and Amharic copy. Photos and exact coordinates are not sent. Avoid including personal or sensitive information in your title or description.

      Generated copy is saved separately and stays private. It does not replace your original details or publish your property. Review facts and translation accuracy before using it.

      Listing fee

      Checking the fee does not charge you or publish your property. Payment is not available in this step.
